Why home daycare

Some dogs thrive with less noise and more individual attention.

A home setting can be a better match for dogs who are comfortable in a household but overwhelmed by a crowded facility. It also gives owners one direct point of contact.

Safety and fit

Clear expectations protect every dog.

  • Current vaccination and health information
  • Accurate disclosure of reactivity, anxiety, escape behavior and bite history
  • Written feeding and medication instructions
  • Emergency contact and veterinarian information
  • Meet-and-greet or trial stay when appropriate
  • Declining a booking when the environment is not a safe fit

FAQ

Before the first stay.

Do dogs need a meet-and-greet?

Yes. It is the simplest way to check comfort, handling and whether the home environment is appropriate.

What should I bring?

Bring labeled food portions, medication with instructions, a leash and any comfort item discussed in advance.

Can you accept every dog?

No. Dogs with behavior, medical or supervision needs that cannot be safely supported in the setting will be declined.

Will I receive photos?

Photo updates are available when they do not interfere with safe supervision. Approved images can also appear in the owner's private gallery.

Do you care for cats?

The primary service is dog daycare and boarding. The planned shop may carry cat food and litter, but cat-care availability must be discussed separately.
